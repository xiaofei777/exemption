# 推免面试29个问题简单回答

这份文档整理了推免面试中常见的29个问题。英文回答使用简单句和常用词，方便记忆。带有方括号的内容，请根据自己的真实情况修改。

## 一 自我介绍

### 1 Please introduce yourself

My name is [name]. I am a student majoring in [major] at [university]. I am interested in [research field]. I am friendly, careful, and willing to learn.

我叫[名字]，是[大学][专业]的学生。我对[研究方向]感兴趣。我性格友好、认真，也愿意学习。

### 2 Please introduce your undergraduate university

My undergraduate university is [university name]. It is in [city]. It has a good study environment and many helpful teachers. I have learned a lot there.

## 二 择校与读研动机

我的本科大学是[大学名称]，位于[城市]。学校有良好的学习环境和很多优秀的老师。我在那里学到了很多。

### 3 Why do you choose our university or department

I choose your university because it has good teachers and strong research in [field]. This department matches my interests. I believe I can learn and grow here.

我选择贵校是因为这里有优秀的老师，在[方向]方面有较强的研究实力。这个院系符合我的兴趣。我相信我可以在这里学习和进步。

### 4 Why do you want to continue your study

I want to learn more about my major. Graduate study can give me more time to do research. It can also help me prepare for my future job.

我想进一步学习自己的专业。研究生学习可以让我有更多时间做科研，也能帮助我为将来的工作做好准备。

### 5 Why did you choose this major

I chose [major] because I am interested in it. I enjoy learning about [topic]. I think this major is useful and has a good future.

我选择[专业]是因为我对它感兴趣。我喜欢学习[主题]方面的内容。我认为这个专业很有用，也有不错的发展前景。

### 6 What do you know about our department

I know that your department has many good teachers. It has strong research in [field]. It also gives students chances to join research projects.

## 三 科研与学术经历

我了解到贵院系有很多优秀的老师，在[方向]方面有较强的科研实力，也会给学生参加科研项目的机会。

### 7 Please introduce your research experience

I joined a research project about [topic]. I was mainly responsible for [your task]. I learned how to find information, use [tool], and solve problems.

我参加过一个关于[主题]的科研项目。我主要负责[你的任务]。我学会了查找资料、使用[工具]和解决问题。

### 8 Please introduce your project

My project was about [topic]. Its main goal was to [goal]. I worked on [your task]. This project taught me how to work with a team.

我的项目是关于[主题]的，主要目标是[目标]。我负责[你的任务]。这个项目让我学会了如何进行团队合作。

### 9 What is your research interest

My research interest is [research direction]. I am interested in [simple research question]. I hope to learn more about this field in the future.

我的研究兴趣是[研究方向]。我对[简单的研究问题]感兴趣。希望将来能进一步学习这个领域。

### 10 Where did your paper come from

My paper came from my research project about [topic]. I found an interesting problem during the project. Then I read papers, did the research, and wrote the paper with my teacher.

我的论文来源于[主题]科研项目。我在项目中发现了一个有趣的问题。之后我阅读论文、开展研究，并和老师一起完成了论文。

### 11 What was your most memorable activity

The most memorable activity was [activity]. I worked with other students and faced some problems. We finished it together, and I learned the importance of teamwork.

## 四 未来规划

我印象最深刻的活动是[活动]。我和其他同学一起完成，并遇到了一些问题。最后我们一起完成了任务，我认识到了团队合作的重要性。

### 12 What would you like to be doing five years after graduation

Five years after graduation, I hope to work in [field or job]. I want to use what I learn in graduate school. I also hope to keep learning and improving myself.

毕业五年后，我希望从事[领域或工作]方面的工作。我想运用研究生阶段学到的知识，也希望继续学习和提升自己。

### 13 What is your future study plan

First, I will learn the basic knowledge of my major. Then I will read papers and join research projects. I will also improve my English and computer skills.

首先，我会学习专业基础知识。然后阅读论文，参加科研项目。我还会提高自己的英语和计算机能力。

### 14 What are your expectations for research results

I hope my research can solve a real problem. I also hope to get a useful result and write a good paper. More importantly, I want to learn from the research process.

我希望我的研究能够解决实际问题，得到有用的结果，并完成一篇好的论文。更重要的是，我希望从科研过程中学到东西。

### 15 Do you plan to pursue a PhD

**有读博计划**

Yes, I would like to study for a PhD if I have the chance. But first, I want to do well in my graduate study and learn more about my research interest.

**暂时没有明确计划**

I do not have a clear plan yet. First, I want to finish my graduate study well. Then I will make a choice based on my interest and future plan.

## 五 个人特质

如果有机会，我愿意继续攻读博士。但现在我首先想做好研究生阶段的学习，进一步了解自己的研究方向。

如果目前还没有明确计划，可以说：我首先想完成好研究生阶段的学习，然后根据自己的兴趣和未来规划作出选择。

### 16 What are your personal strengths

I am responsible and patient. When I start a task, I try to finish it well. I also like learning new things.

我有责任心，也很有耐心。开始一项任务后，我会努力把它做好。我也喜欢学习新事物。

### 17 What is your greatest weakness

Sometimes I spend too much time on small details. Now I make a clear plan and set a time limit. This helps me work better and faster.

有时候我会在小细节上花太多时间。现在我会制定清晰的计划，并规定时间。这能帮助我更好、更快地完成任务。

### 18 What is your greatest achievement

I am proud of [achievement]. It was not easy, but I kept working and finished it. This experience gave me more confidence.

我最自豪的成就是[成就]。这件事并不容易，但我坚持完成了它。这段经历让我更有信心。

### 19 What role do you prefer in a team

I prefer to be a responsible team member. I listen to others and finish my work on time. When needed, I also share ideas and help the team.

我更喜欢做一个有责任心的团队成员。我会听取别人的意见，并按时完成自己的工作。需要时，我也会分享想法并帮助团队。

### 20 Use three words to describe yourself

I would use three words: responsible, patient, and positive. I take my work seriously and try to find solutions.

我会用三个词形容自己：负责、耐心、积极。我认真对待工作，并努力寻找解决办法。

## 六 生活与通识

### 21 What are your hobbies

In my free time, I like reading and listening to music. Sometimes I exercise or take a walk. I also read technology news and learn new tools. These activities help me relax and keep learning.

空闲时间，我喜欢阅读和听音乐。有时我也会运动或散步。我还会阅读科技新闻，学习新的工具。这些活动能让我放松，也能让我继续学习。

### 22 What is your favorite movie or book

My favorite movie is *Forrest Gump*. I like it because the story is simple and meaningful. The main character is kind and never gives up. It tells me to stay positive and keep trying.

我最喜欢的电影是《阿甘正传》。我喜欢它是因为故事简单而有意义。主人公善良，而且从不放弃。它告诉我要保持积极并不断努力。

### 23 What is your favorite season Why

My favorite season is autumn. The weather is cool and comfortable, and the view is beautiful. I like walking and studying in this season. Autumn makes me feel peaceful.

我最喜欢秋天。秋天天气凉爽舒适，景色也很美。我喜欢在这个季节散步和学习。秋天让我感到平静。

### 24 Please describe your hometown

My hometown is [city or province]. It is famous for [food or place]. The people there are friendly, and the life is comfortable. There are many good memories in my hometown. I love it very much.

我的家乡是[城市或省份]，它以[食物或地方]而闻名。那里的人很友好，生活也很舒适。我的家乡有很多美好的回忆。我非常喜欢那里。

### 25 How do you deal with stress

When I feel stressed, I make a simple plan first. Then I divide a big task into small tasks and do them one by one. I also take a walk, listen to music, or talk with friends. These things help me calm down.

感到有压力时，我会先制定一个简单的计划。然后把大任务分成小任务，一件一件完成。我也会散步、听音乐或和朋友聊天。这些事情能帮助我平静下来。

### 26 What is the difference between undergraduate and graduate study

Undergraduate study focuses more on basic knowledge and classes. Graduate study focuses more on research and personal work. In graduate school, students need to read papers, ask questions, and solve problems by themselves.

本科阶段更重视基础知识和课堂学习。研究生阶段更重视科研和独立工作。在研究生阶段，学生需要阅读论文、提出问题，并自己解决问题。

### 27 What qualities should a researcher have

A researcher should be curious, patient, and careful. He or she should ask good questions and check the details. A researcher should also be honest when doing experiments. Teamwork is important too.

研究者应该有好奇心、耐心和细心。要善于提出问题，也要认真检查细节。做实验时还要诚实。团队合作也很重要。

### 28 What is more important Choice or hard work

I think both are important. A good choice gives us the right direction, and hard work helps us reach the goal. We should first choose a good direction and then work hard. If the choice is wrong, we can learn and change our plan.

我认为两者都很重要。正确的选择能给我们方向，努力能帮助我们实现目标。我们应该先选择正确的方向，然后努力奋斗。如果选择错了，我们也可以吸取教训并改变计划。

### 29 What would you do if you failed this interview

If I fail this interview, I will accept the result calmly. I will think about my weak points and ask for advice. Then I will improve myself and prepare for the next chance. I will not give up on my goal.

如果这次面试失败，我会平静地接受结果。我会思考自己的不足，并请教别人。然后提升自己，为下一次机会做准备。我不会放弃自己的目标。
## 背诵小提示
1. 先记住每个回答的三句话意思，再背英文。
2. 把方括号内容换成自己的真实信息。
3. 每个问题准备一个具体例子，老师追问时更容易回答。
4. 说慢一点，句子短一点，表达清楚最重要。
