# 自我介绍：1 分钟

**Question: Please introduce yourself.**  
（请介绍一下你自己。）

> Good morning, professors. My name is Feiyang Chen. I am a senior student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at Wuhan University of Science and Technology. I rank 16th out of 207 students, in the top 7.7 percent.
>
> I am interested in using AI to solve real problems. I led CamTalk, a real-time vision and voice dialogue system. I made a simple key-frame method. It cut data uploads by about 80 percent when the user was silent. I also helped build a streaming speech-to-text, LLM, and text-to-speech system. Its full delay was less than 0.5 seconds.
>
> I also improved a YOLOv8 steel-defect detector with CBAM attention. Its mAP at IoU 0.5 rose from 77.5 percent to about 80 to 81 percent.
>
> These projects gave me practice in error analysis, model tests, and system building. In graduate school, I hope to study large-model reasoning, AI agents, and multimodal learning. Thank you.

## 中文翻译

各位老师早上好。我叫陈飞扬，是武汉科技大学计算机科学与技术专业的大四学生。我的成绩排名是 207 名学生中的第 16 名，位于专业前 7.7%。我对用人工智能解决实际问题很感兴趣。我负责过 CamTalk，这是一个实时视觉和语音对话系统。我设计了一个简单的关键帧方法，使用户安静时的数据上传量减少了约 80%。我还参与构建了语音转文字、大语言模型和文字转语音的流式系统。它的整体延迟低于 0.5 秒。我还使用 CBAM 注意力机制改进了 YOLOv8 钢材缺陷检测器。在 IoU 为 0.5 时，它的 mAP 从 77.5% 提高到约 80%～81%。这些项目让我积累了误差分析、模型测试和系统开发经验。研究生阶段，我希望学习大模型推理、人工智能智能体和多模态学习。谢谢各位老师。
