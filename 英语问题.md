# 保研面试英语系列问题

> 以下回答使用简单词汇和短句，方便记忆。每个回答后附中文翻译。

## 1. 自我介绍类

### 1. What can you tell me about yourself?
**Answer:**
My name is Chen Feiyang. I study Computer Science at Wuhan University of Science and Technology. My rank is 16 out of 207. I have experience in AI, big models, and software development. I hope to study big models and intelligent agents in graduate school.

**翻译：**
我叫陈飞扬，就读于武汉科技大学计算机科学与技术专业。我的成绩排名是16/207。我有人工智能、大模型和软件开发方面的实践经历。研究生阶段，我希望学习大模型和智能体。

### 2. Please introduce your undergraduate university.
**Answer:**
My university is Wuhan University of Science and Technology. It is a university with a good learning environment. The school provides many chances for students to practice and do research. I have learned computer science and taken part in several projects here.

**翻译：**
我的本科院校是武汉科技大学。这是一所学习环境良好的大学。学校为学生提供了很多实践和科研机会。我在这里学习了计算机科学，也参加了几个项目。

## 2. 择校与读研动机类

### 3. Why do you choose to study in our university or department?
**Answer:**
Your university has strong teachers and good research resources. Your research direction is close to my interests, such as AI, big models, and computer vision. I hope to learn from your teachers and do useful research here.

**翻译：**
贵校师资力量强，科研资源好。贵校的研究方向与我的兴趣接近，比如人工智能、大模型和计算机视觉。我希望在这里向老师学习，做一些有用的研究。

### 4. Why do you choose to continue your further study?
**Answer:**
My undergraduate study gave me a good start, but I still need more theory and research training. Graduate study can help me learn how to find problems and solve them. It can also help me improve my research and coding skills.

**翻译：**
本科阶段的学习为我打下了基础，但我还需要更多理论和科研训练。研究生学习可以帮助我学会发现问题和解决问题，也能提升我的科研和编程能力。

### 5. Why did you choose this major?
**Answer:**
I chose computer science because I like technology and problem solving. I enjoy turning an idea into a working program. AI also has many real-world uses, so I want to study it more deeply.

**翻译：**
我选择计算机科学，是因为我喜欢技术和解决问题。我喜欢把一个想法变成可以运行的程序。人工智能有很多实际用途，所以我想继续深入学习。

### 6. What do you know about our department?
**Answer:**
I know that your department has many good teachers and research teams. It works on important areas such as artificial intelligence, computer vision, and software systems. I have read about your teachers' research, and I am very interested in it.

**翻译：**
我了解到贵系有很多优秀的老师和科研团队，研究人工智能、计算机视觉和软件系统等重要方向。我阅读过一些老师的研究介绍，对这些方向很感兴趣。

## 3. 科研与学术经历类

### 7. Please introduce your research experience in English.
**Answer:**
I have worked on two main AI projects. In a steel defect project, I used the CBAM attention method in YOLOv8. The mAP@0.5 increased from 77.5% to about 80%–81%. I also worked on a real-time multimodal AI agent and learned about data sampling, model calls, and system testing.

**翻译：**
我主要做过两个人工智能项目。在钢材缺陷检测项目中，我在YOLOv8中加入了CBAM注意力机制，使mAP@0.5从77.5%提高到约80%—81%。我还参与了实时多模态人工智能智能体项目，学习了数据采样、模型调用和系统测试。

### 8. Please introduce your project in English.
**Answer:**
My project is called CamTalk. It is a real-time AI agent that can see, hear, and talk. I designed a key-frame sampling method, so the upload data in quiet time was reduced by about 80%. The system delay was less than 0.5 seconds.

**翻译：**
我的项目叫CamTalk。它是一个可以看、听和说话的实时人工智能智能体。我设计了关键帧采样方法，使安静时的数据上传量减少了约80%。系统延迟小于0.5秒。

### 9. What is your research direction or interest?
**Answer:**
I am interested in big models, intelligent agents, and multimodal AI. I want to study how to make AI systems more useful, stable, and easy to use. I am also interested in AI safety and computer vision.

**翻译：**
我对大模型、智能体和多模态人工智能感兴趣。我希望研究如何让人工智能系统更有用、更稳定、更容易使用。我也对人工智能安全和计算机视觉感兴趣。

### 10. Where did your paper come from?
**Answer:**
My research idea came from a real problem in steel production. Cracks were hard to detect because their texture was weak. I studied the wrong results, added the CBAM method, and checked it with comparison experiments. At present, I am still improving my research and writing skills.

**翻译：**
我的研究想法来自钢材生产中的一个实际问题。由于裂纹纹理较弱，所以很难检测。我分析了错误结果，加入CBAM方法，并通过对比实验进行验证。目前，我还在继续提高自己的科研和写作能力。

### 11. What was the most memorable activity you participated in during undergraduate studies?
**Answer:**
The most memorable activity was the steel defect detection project. I found a real problem, designed a solution, and tested it with data. The project won a school silver award, and the technology was used by a company. I learned a lot from this process.

**翻译：**
本科期间最难忘的是钢材缺陷检测项目。我发现了一个实际问题，设计了解决方法，并用数据进行测试。这个项目获得了校级银奖，相关技术也被企业采用。我从这个过程中学到了很多。

## 4. 未来规划类

**Answer:**

**翻译：**

### 26. What is the difference between undergraduate and graduate studies?
**Answer:**
Undergraduate study focuses more on basic knowledge. Graduate study focuses more on research and independent work. Graduate students need to ask questions and find answers by themselves.

**翻译：**
本科学习更重视基础知识。研究生学习更重视科研和独立完成工作。研究生需要自己提出问题并寻找答案。

### 27. What qualities should a researcher possess?
**Answer:**
A researcher should be patient, careful, and honest. He or she should be able to find problems and test ideas. Good teamwork is also important.

**翻译：**
科研人员应该有耐心、细心并且诚实。他们应该能够发现问题并验证想法。良好的团队合作也很重要。

### 28. What do you think is more important, choice or hard work?
**Answer:**

**翻译：**
我认为两者都重要。好的选择能给我们正确的方向，努力能帮助我们达到目标。没有努力，好的选择也不够。

**翻译：**
我会接受结果，找出自己需要改进的地方。然后继续学习，为下一次机会做准备。失败对我来说不是终点。

### 30. You have done many projects. How do you balance your time?
**Answer:**
I make a clear plan before I start. I list the important tasks and finish them first. I divide my time between study, projects, and rest. When I work on a project, I focus on one task at a time. This helps me finish my work on time.

**翻译：**
开始之前，我会制定清晰的计划。我会列出重要任务，优先完成它们。我会合理安排学习、项目和休息的时间。做项目时，我一次专注于一项任务。这能帮助我按时完成工作。
