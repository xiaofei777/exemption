# 动机、规划与综合问题

## Why do you choose our university?

> I am applying to [University] for three reasons. First, it has a strong research group in [research direction]. Second, Professor [Professor]'s work on [topic or paper] matches my experience in multimodal agents, computer vision, and AI engineering. Third, I want to move from building systems to asking research questions and testing ideas carefully. I believe your group can give me the theory and research training I need. I can also help with data, experiments, and prototypes.

我申请 [University] 有三个原因。第一，贵校在 [research direction] 方向有很强的研究团队。第二，[Professor] 教授关于 [topic or paper] 的研究与我在多模态智能体、计算机视觉和 AI 工程方面的经历相符。第三，我希望从构建系统进步到提出研究问题并认真验证想法。我相信贵课题组能提供我需要的理论和科研训练。我也可以在数据、实验和原型开发方面提供帮助。

## What is your plan for graduate study?

> In my first year, I will strengthen math, machine learning, deep learning, and academic English. I will read key papers and repeat several methods. Later, I hope to choose one clear problem in large-model reasoning, AI agents, or multimodal learning. I will run fair tests, study failure cases, and learn paper writing. In the long term, I hope to publish solid work and may pursue a PhD if my interest and ability grow.

第一年，我会加强数学、机器学习、深度学习和学术英语。我会阅读重要论文并复现一些方法。之后，我希望在大模型推理、人工智能智能体或多模态学习中选择一个明确问题。我会进行公平测试、研究失败案例并学习论文写作。长期来看，我希望发表扎实的成果；如果兴趣和能力不断提高，也可能攻读博士学位。

## What are your strengths and weaknesses?

> My strengths are responsibility, coding ability, and persistence when debugging. I break a large task into small parts and check each part with tests. One weakness is that I sometimes spend too long on small details. I am improving by setting priorities, finishing a minimum result first, and leaving time for tests and review.

我的优点是责任心、编码能力和调试时的坚持。我会把大任务分成小部分，并用测试检查每一部分。我的一个缺点是有时在小细节上花费太多时间。我正在通过设定优先级、先完成最低可用结果，并留出测试和复盘时间来改进。

## How do you deal with pressure or failure?

> I first separate facts, guesses, and actions. For a model problem, I check data, labels, measures, and error cases before changing the model. For a schedule problem, I find the key task and talk to teammates early. This helped me stay calm when my first detector did poorly on crack defects.

我会先区分事实、猜测和行动。遇到模型问题时，我会在修改模型前检查数据、标签、指标和错误案例。遇到进度问题时，我会找出关键任务并尽早和队友沟通。第一次检测器在裂纹缺陷上表现不好时，这种方法帮助我保持冷静。

## Why should we choose you with limited publications?

> My paper record is still limited, and I want to be honest about that. But I have finished several full projects. I know how to connect a real problem with error analysis, a method change, and a fair test. I also know I need stronger theory, paper reading, careful experiments, and academic writing. I am ready to learn these skills and help the group with reliable code and careful tests.

我的论文成果还比较少，我愿意诚实面对这一点。但我已经完成了几个完整项目。我知道如何把实际问题、误差分析、方法改动和公平测试联系起来。我也知道自己需要加强理论、论文阅读、严谨实验和学术写作。我愿意学习这些技能，并用可靠的代码和认真细致的测试帮助课题组。

## What is your English level?

> I have passed CET-4. My spoken English is still improving, especially when I answer without preparation. I practice by reading AI papers aloud, giving short summaries, and recording my answers. With preparation, I can understand technical material. I am working to speak more smoothly and clearly.

我已经通过大学英语四级。我的英语口语还在提高，尤其是没有准备时的回答能力。我通过朗读 AI 论文、做简短总结和录音来练习。经过准备后，我能够理解专业材料。我正在努力让口语更流利、更清晰。
