# 英语面试生活与常见问题

> 以下回答使用简单句和常用词，方便背诵。每个回答后附中文翻译。

## 1. Please introduce your hometown.

**Answer:**
My hometown is in Hubei Province. It has a long history and many delicious foods. The people there are friendly and warm. Life there is comfortable and peaceful. I often go home during holidays, and I enjoy spending time with my family.

**翻译：**
我的家乡在湖北省。那里历史悠久，也有很多美食。那里的人友好热情。那里的生活舒适而平静。我经常在假期回家，喜欢和家人一起度过时光。

## 2. Please introduce your university.

**Answer:**
I study at Wuhan University of Science and Technology. It has a good study environment and many helpful teachers. My major is Computer Science and Technology. I have learned computer science and taken part in several projects there. The university has helped me build a good foundation.

**翻译：**
我就读于武汉科技大学。学校有良好的学习环境和很多热心的老师。我的专业是计算机科学与技术。我在那里学习了计算机科学，也参加了几个项目。这所大学帮助我打下了良好的基础。

## 3. What are your hobbies?

**Answer:**
In my free time, I like listening to music, walking, and reading technology news. Sometimes I read books or watch useful videos. These activities help me relax after study. I also enjoy learning new software tools because they are useful for my projects.

**翻译：**
空闲时间，我喜欢听音乐、散步和阅读科技新闻。有时我会看书或观看有用的视频。这些活动能让我在学习后放松。我也喜欢学习新的软件工具，因为它们对我的项目很有用。

## 4. What is your research plan?

**Answer:**
First, I will learn more basic knowledge in machine learning and computer science. Then I will read papers and repeat some experiments. I also want to improve my English and writing skills. My main interests are big models, intelligent agents, and multimodal AI. I hope to finish a useful research project.

**翻译：**
首先，我会学习机器学习和计算机科学方面的基础知识。然后阅读论文，复现一些实验。我还想提高英语和写作能力。我主要感兴趣的方向是大模型、智能体和多模态人工智能。我希望完成一个有用的科研项目。

## 5. What is the impact of AI?

**Answer:**
AI makes our work faster and more convenient. It can help people in education, medicine, and industry. It can also help us find information and solve simple problems. But AI may bring problems, such as data safety and wrong information. We should use AI carefully and check important results.

**翻译：**
人工智能让我们的工作更快、更方便。它可以帮助教育、医疗和工业等领域。它还可以帮助我们查找信息和解决简单问题。但人工智能也会带来一些问题，比如数据安全和错误信息。我们应该谨慎使用人工智能，并检查重要结果。

## 6. Please introduce your paper or research project.

**Answer:**
My research project is about steel defect detection. In this project, I studied six kinds of defects. I found that cracks were difficult to detect because their texture was weak. So I added the CBAM attention method to YOLOv8. The mAP increased from 77.5% to about 80%–81%.

**翻译：**
我的科研项目是钢材缺陷检测。在这个项目中，我研究了六类缺陷。我发现裂纹很难检测，因为它的纹理较弱。因此，我在 YOLOv8 中加入了 CBAM 注意力机制。mAP 从 77.5% 提高到了约 80%—81%。

## 7. What is your weakness?

**Answer:**
Sometimes I spend too much time on small details. This may make my work slower. Now I make a clear plan and set a time limit for each task. I finish the main work first and then check the details. This helps me work better and faster.

**翻译：**
有时候我会在小细节上花太多时间，这可能会让我的工作变慢。现在我会制定清晰的计划，并为每项任务规定时间。我会先完成主要工作，再检查细节。这能帮助我更好、更快地完成任务。

## 8. What are your strengths?

**Answer:**
I am responsible, patient, and willing to learn. When I start a task, I try to finish it carefully. I can turn an idea into a working program. I also stay calm when I meet problems. In addition, I work well with other people.

**翻译：**
我有责任心、有耐心，也愿意学习。开始一项任务后，我会认真完成。我能把一个想法变成可以运行的程序。遇到问题时，我也能保持冷静。此外，我能够和他人良好合作。

## 9. What research method do you often use?

**Answer:**
I often use literature review, experiments, and error analysis. First, I read papers and find a useful problem. Then I design an experiment and compare the results. If the result is not good, I check the data and the method. Finally, I find the reason for the errors and improve the method.

**翻译：**
我经常使用文献阅读、实验和误差分析。首先，我阅读论文并发现有价值的问题。然后设计实验并比较结果。如果结果不好，我会检查数据和方法。最后分析错误产生的原因，并改进方法。

## 10. Why do you want to continue your study?

**Answer:**
I want to learn more about my major and improve my research skills. My undergraduate study gave me a good foundation, but I still need more training. Graduate study can help me read papers, design experiments, and solve real problems. I also want to work with good teachers and students.

**翻译：**
我想进一步学习自己的专业，也想提高科研能力。本科阶段的学习为我打下了基础，但我还需要更多训练。研究生学习可以帮助我阅读论文、设计实验并解决实际问题。我也希望和优秀的老师、同学一起学习。

## 11. What difficulty did you meet in a competition?

**Answer:**
In one competition, our project had many technical problems. The system was sometimes slow and unstable. We checked the code, discussed with teammates, and tested it many times. We also divided the work clearly. Finally, we solved the main problems and finished the project on time.

**翻译：**
在一次比赛中，我们的项目遇到了很多技术问题。系统有时运行缓慢，也不稳定。我们检查代码、和队友讨论，并进行了多次测试。我们还明确分配了工作。最后，我们解决了主要问题，并按时完成了项目。

## 12. How do you deal with failure?

**Answer:**
I first accept the result calmly. Then I think about my weak points and find the reasons. I may ask teachers or friends for advice. After that, I make a new plan and try again. I believe failure can help me learn and make progress.

**翻译：**
我会先平静地接受结果。然后思考自己的不足，找到问题的原因。我也会向老师或朋友请教。之后制定新的计划，再次尝试。我认为失败可以帮助我学习和进步。

## 13. How do you work in a team?

**Answer:**
I listen to other people first and understand the team plan. Then I share my ideas and finish my own work on time. If I meet a problem, I discuss it with my teammates. If someone needs help, I am willing to help. I think clear communication is very important.

**翻译：**
我会先听取别人的意见，了解团队计划。然后分享自己的想法，并按时完成自己的工作。如果遇到问题，我会和队友讨论。如果有人需要帮助，我也愿意提供帮助。我认为清晰的沟通非常重要。

## 14. What is your favorite project?

**Answer:**
My favorite project is CamTalk. It is a real-time multimodal AI agent that can see, hear, and talk. I helped design the key-frame sampling method. It reduced the upload data by about 80% during quiet time. This project taught me how to turn an idea into a working system.

**翻译：**
我最喜欢的项目是 CamTalk。它是一个可以看、听和说话的实时多模态人工智能智能体。我参与设计了关键帧采样方法，使安静时的数据上传量减少了约 80%。这个项目让我学会了如何把一个想法做成可运行的系统。

**Answer:**
I hope to work in AI research or AI engineering. I want to use what I learn to solve real problems. I also want to keep learning new technology and improve my skills. If I have the chance, I hope to work on big models and intelligent agents. I want to make useful systems for people.

我希望从事人工智能科研或人工智能工程工作。我想运用所学知识解决实际问题。我也希望继续学习新技术并提高自己的能力。如果有机会，我希望研究大模型和智能体。我想为人们做出有用的系统。
