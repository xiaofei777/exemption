# 自我介绍：3 分钟

> Good morning, professors. My name is Feiyang Chen. I am a senior student in Computer Science and Technology at Wuhan University of Science and Technology. I rank 16th out of 207 students, in the top 7.7 percent. I have received the National Encouragement Scholarship and several university awards.

各位老师早上好。我叫陈飞扬，是武汉科技大学计算机科学与技术专业的大四学生。我的成绩排名是 207 名学生中的第 16 名，位于专业前 7.7%。我获得过国家励志奖学金和几项校级荣誉。

> My main interest is using AI in real life. From December 2025 to May 2026, I was a research intern at Wuhan Jinyinhu Laboratory. I worked on a software supply-chain security platform and an LLM application. I built over fifteen ECharts charts, such as a threat map and CVE trend chart. I also helped design TcodeAI, a tool that gives repair advice for software problems. I learned that a useful AI system needs good data, error handling, and a clear interface, not only a model.

我最主要的兴趣是把人工智能用于现实场景。2025 年 12 月到 2026 年 5 月，我在武汉金银湖实验室做科研实习。我参与了软件供应链安全平台和大语言模型应用的开发，制作了 15 个以上的 ECharts 图表，例如威胁地图和 CVE 趋势图。我还参与设计了 TcodeAI，这是一个为软件问题提供修复建议的工具。我认识到，一个有用的 AI 系统不仅需要模型，还需要良好的数据、错误处理和清晰的界面。

> I was also the leader of CamTalk, a real-time vision and voice dialogue system. It was made for a safe setting where data stays local. I used voice activity detection and pixel differences to choose key frames. When the user was silent, or the picture changed very little, the system did not send repeated frames. This cut uploads by about 80 percent. I also helped connect speech recognition, an LLM, and speech synthesis in a seven-node streaming system. By sending text and audio in small parts, we kept the full delay below 0.5 seconds.

我还担任 CamTalk 实时视觉和语音对话系统的负责人。这个系统用于需要数据保存在本地的安全场景。我使用语音活动检测和像素差异来选择关键帧。当用户安静或画面变化很小时，系统不会发送重复帧，因此上传量减少了约 80%。我还参与把语音识别、大语言模型和语音合成连接到七节点流式系统中。通过分小段发送文字和音频，我们将整体延迟控制在 0.5 秒以内。

> In another project, I worked on hot-rolled steel defect detection. On the NEU-DET dataset, cracks were the hardest class because their texture was weak and their edges were unclear. I added CBAM attention to the Neck of YOLOv8. With the same data and settings, mAP at IoU 0.5 rose from 77.5 percent to about 80 to 81 percent. Crack AP rose from 52.2 percent to about 57 to 60 percent. One image took about 3 milliseconds to process.

在另一个项目中，我研究了热轧带钢缺陷检测。在 NEU-DET 数据集上，裂纹最难检测，因为纹理较弱、边缘不清晰。我把 CBAM 注意力加入 YOLOv8 的 Neck。在相同数据和设置下，IoU 为 0.5 时的 mAP 从 77.5% 提升到约 80%～81%，裂纹 AP 从 52.2% 提升到约 57%～60%。处理一张图像大约需要 3 毫秒。

> These projects taught me to connect coding with careful tests. I still need stronger theory, paper reading, experiment design, and academic writing. I hope to join a group working on large-model reasoning, AI agents, or multimodal learning. In the short term, I will build my basics and repeat key papers. In the long term, I hope to do my own research and build reliable AI systems. Thank you for listening.

这些项目让我学会把代码实现和严谨测试结合起来。我还需要加强理论、论文阅读、实验设计和学术写作。我希望加入研究大模型推理、人工智能智能体或多模态学习的课题组。短期内，我会打好基础并复现重要论文。长期来看，我希望开展自己的研究，构建可靠的 AI 系统。感谢各位老师的聆听。
